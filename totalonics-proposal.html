1. Executive Summary

M-Link System (M) Sdn Bhd stands as one of Malaysia's premier IT distribution partners, serving a vast network of over 7,000 active resellers nationwide. Your digital storefront is the primary gateway through which thousands of business customers discover, evaluate, and procure technology products every day.

This proposal outlines a rapid, low-risk frontend redesign of your existing 24/7 online shop — delivering a premium, corporate-grade UI/UX that reflects the scale and authority of M-Link's market position. The approach is deliberately non-disruptive: backend systems, ERP integrations, and live transaction pipelines remain untouched throughout the process.

2. The Vision

Premium Corporate Aesthetic

A refined visual identity that communicates trust, scale, and technological sophistication to enterprise buyers.

Seamless Reseller Journey

Streamlined navigation, faster product discovery, and an intuitive checkout flow designed for high-volume B2B buyers.

Zero Downtime Deployment

Your 24/7 shop stays live throughout. We build on isolated staging environments with a phased, risk-free cutover.

3. Scope of Work — Phase 1

3.1

UI/UX Design

High-fidelity Figma prototyping covering the complete customer journey — Homepage, Catalog Listing, Product Detail Pages, and Reseller Registration flows. Every screen is designed for clarity, speed, and enterprise-grade trust signals.

3.2

Custom Frontend Development

Building a responsive, high-performance enterprise interface using modern Headless architecture (Next.js / React) that connects safely to the existing backend. No monolithic redesign — a layered, API-first approach that keeps your current systems intact.

3.3

System Integration Discovery

A mandatory technical audit to verify how the current online shop communicates with Microsoft Dynamics and downstream ERP systems. This discovery phase identifies data flow paths, sync mechanisms, and potential conflict points — ensuring zero data loss and no ERP disruption during the frontend transition.

4. Development Standards

All development is conducted on isolated Git branches within dedicated staging environments that mirror production. No code reaches the live shop without passing through a structured review pipeline. Production deployment occurs only after rigorous User Acceptance Testing (UAT) and explicit written approval from the M-Link technical team. Every commit is version-tracked, every change is reversible, and every milestone is sign-off gated.
